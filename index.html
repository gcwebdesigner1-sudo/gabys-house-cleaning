Questions people ask

How much does house cleaning cost?

Every quote is free and based on the size of your home, its condition, and how often you want it cleaned. Call or text (208) 391-0310 and Gaby will give you a straight number, usually within one business day.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Caldwell, Idaho and clean homes and businesses in Caldwell, Nampa, Middleton, Meridian, Boise, Kuna, Eagle, Garden City and Star.

Do you bring your own cleaning supplies?

Yes. Gaby brings professional cleaning products and tools to every visit. If you prefer certain products or have allergies, say so and she will use what works for your home.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Gaby's House Cleaning LLC is licensed and insured.

How often should I have my house cleaned?

Most households book every two weeks. Families with pets or small kids often choose weekly, and some homes do fine with monthly. A deep clean first, then regular visits, keeps the work and the price down.

What's the difference between a regular clean and a deep clean?

A regular clean keeps kitchens, bathrooms, floors and surfaces in shape. A deep clean goes further: baseboards, vents, grout, cabinet fronts, light fixtures and buildup that a routine visit does not reach.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday 8am to 6pm and Saturday 9am to 3pm. You can text or send a quote request any time.
